## Result

**Incomplete: the 400 m horizontal distance bound stopped the test.** Game time
03:32.102, lap 1/1; controller duration 242.46 seconds. The drone followed the
long descent, reaching 139.15 m below launch elevation. No detected impact,
estimated collision, camera outage or controller deadline failure. The runner
automatically paused the game when the distance bound was exceeded.

Full standard brain/gameplay video and all raw telemetry are preserved under
`runs/race-completion-20260922/pit-cue-01*`. This is not a race finish and cannot
be excluded when describing the attempted unseen evaluations.

The common envelope for subsequent full-race tests was expanded to 2000 m
horizontal distance and 250 m above launch, retaining the 10 m/s speed bound.
These values only stop the runner; they do not enter its steering observations.
No navigation, perception, speed-assistance or neural parameters changed after
this attempt. To keep a strict untouched evaluation after changing the protocol,
the next full-race test uses a different previously untested course.
