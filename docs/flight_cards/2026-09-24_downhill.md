# Straw Bale downhill: yaw weave and arch-top clearance — 2026-09-24

The user saw the drone "arch side to side" on the downhill. Logs of the descent
(`straw-brain07-02`, `straw-fast6-arc-01`, `straw-fast6-03`) show why: after the
ring had stayed clipped at the bottom edge, centred, for 1.5 s, the edge sweep
(meant to separate a ring overhead from one behind) took over the yaw at
1 rad/s and re-decided its direction from the bearing every ~5 degrees, so the
drone yawed bang-bang +-7 degrees every 0.1-0.25 s until the ring reappeared.
Liftoff clamps rings behind the drone to the top edge, so a bottom clip is
never "behind": the sweep now applies only to top clips, and its direction is
latched per episode. Live (`straw-brain07-nosweep-01`), the share of downhill time
at more than 0.5 rad/s yaw rate fell from 37% to 4% and yaw-stick spread from
0.21 to 0.07.

That run then hit the top beam of the second descending arch in lap 2 (race
3:21.9). Every earlier run crossed that arch at 6.75-7.14 m; this one at
7.62/7.35 m. The sweep had been letting the ring into view now and then, when
the pilot aims straight at it; without it the ring stayed clipped and the drone
followed a slope only just steeper than the clipped edge ray, i.e. it approached
the lower ring from above. The PD's lap-3 crash on the first arch (13.32 m against
12.9-13.2 m on clean laps) is the same margin. A ring that stays clipped below
while the drone follows that slope must be steeper still, so the descent margin
now grows with the clip's duration (5 degrees + 4 degrees/s, capped at 20 degrees).

Surrogate check (16 synthetic courses, 6 m/s): PD 14/16 finished (13/16 before),
crashes unchanged at 2, the steep course where the PD circled above a low gate
dropped from 93.8 s to 63.0 s; brain-07 15/16 (14/16), 0 crashes, paired finish
-1.45 s, stick change per tick 0.0040 -> 0.0027. The live check of the steeper
descent was interrupted: the machine-wide virtual gamepad was reaching the
user's own game, so the flight and pad were stopped. It remains to be flown.
