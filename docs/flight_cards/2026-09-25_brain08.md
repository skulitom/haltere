# fast-brain-08 candidate on Straw Bale — 2026-09-25

Goal: the downhill fix from the [downhill card](2026-09-24_downhill.md) needs a
brain that brakes and sinks when the pilot asks. brain-08 was re-distilled under
that pilot. Developed on branch `brain08`, merged into main at `09898a1` (main now
flies the downhill-fixed pilot; brain-07 is superseded).

## Why brain-07 could not follow the fixed pilot

Open-loop replays of `straw-brain07-steep-02` against FastMotorPD on the exact
recorded inputs, surrogate plant swaps from the live crash states and training
coverage checks, each re-checked by an independent reviewer:

- Not a plant mismatch: the measured surrogate reproduces the live descent
  within 0.02 m/s, and brain-07 fails the same requests there. The 16-course
  finish count hid it because a pass anywhere in the 3 m sphere counts.
- Braking was held back by the readout's ridge of 10 (pitch gap to the
  teacher +0.78 on the steep, slow ticks); a lower ridge recovers it.
- Sink: the goal neurons stop responding beyond a vertical goal of about 1 m,
  so throttle was flat for requests steeper than -1 m/s.

## Recipe

`fast_motor_tracking` gained `--vertical-goal-seconds` (0.4: the runtime reads
it from the checkpoint), `--sink-weight` (samples requesting >= 1.5 m/s sink),
`--smooth` (penalty on the command change over one 10 ms tick) and `--resolve`
(refit a run's saved data). Base run `runs/fast-brain-08-vgs04` (5 DAgger rounds
under the fixed pilot, ridge 0.3, sink weight 10, smooth 3); finalists refit from
it. Only readout rows 0-2 change. Low ridge (0.03) raised closed-loop chatter even
with heavy smoothing (the open-loop smoothness proxy was misleading).

Surrogate, 16 standard courses under the fixed pilot:

r0.3/s30 (`runs/fast-brain-08-vgs04-s10r03m30/candidate.pt`) was flown.

## Live (Straw Bale / Field Day, 6 m/s, original `[Copy] New Drone`, hidden seat)

| Run | Outcome |
|---|---|
| `straw-brain08-01` | Lap 1, 76 s: slid down the hillside before the arches and struck the ground |
| `straw-brain08-02` | Slope support rule added (below). Lap 1 complete, arches cleared; lap 2, 164 s: hilltop crash |
| `straw-brain08-03` | Lap 1, 59 s: same hilltop crash |

- **The downhill weave is gone.** Lap-1 descent of `straw-brain08-02` against
  `straw-brain07-02`: yaw rate above 0.5 rad/s 3% of the time (32%), yaw-stick
  spread 0.08 (0.20), roll command change per tick 0.0030 (0.0090), pitch 0.0044
  (0.0081); the descending arches were crossed 0.7 m lower than the beam brain-07
  hit, and the descent took 24 s (26 s).
- **Slope contact.** In `straw-brain08-01` the issued throttle stayed 0.3-0.4
  below hover while the drone sank 0.5 of a requested 1.25 m/s and slowed from 4.4
  to 2.1 m/s: it was sliding down the hill. The support rule only fired when the
  sink stopped. It now also fires on a sink shortfall of 0.4 m/s with throttle
  0.2 below hover for 0.6 s (commit `e65b5af`); it lifted the drone off that
  hillside twice in `straw-brain08-02`, with no free-air trigger in the surrogate.
- **Hilltop.** brain-08 and the fast PD climb the hill on the pilot's requested
  line, about 1.5 m lower than brain-07 (which over-climbed: +0.66 m/s for a
  +0.31 request). There the ring marker sits against bright straw and drops out
  34-69% of the time (brain-07 3-6%). The pilot coasts, then brakes to a stop from
  5.5 m/s; with the ring back at the bottom edge the teacher asks for roll swings
  of about +-0.8, which brain-08 follows about 0.1 s late: +-50 degrees of bank
  0.7 m above the plateau, then ground contact. The fast PD went through a similar
  cue-loss sequence at this spot in `straw-fast6-03` (earlier pilot) without
  contact; it has not been flown live with this pilot.
